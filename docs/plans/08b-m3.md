# Step 8b: M-3, a pendant tree inside a ring

Branch `feature/m3-deferred` off `dev`. STATUS step 8b; design: `docs/design.md` open point "door
capacity" and build order.

## Problem

M-3 (40 rooms) fails all 5 layout seeds, stuck on tree chain `[26,25,19,17,27,20,31,34]` after
2.6 s/seed. Step 8 (hub sizes) did not change it.

## Investigation (2026-10-27, scratch scripts, layout seeds 1–5)

Chains of M-3 (room numbers, `type/degree`):

| # | kind  | rooms                                       | touches |
|---|-------|---------------------------------------------|---------|
| 0 | cycle | 16 9 15                                     |         |
| 1 | tree  | 3 (hub, 7) 2 4 8 5 1 14 24                  | 0       |
| 2 | cycle | 6 11 10 18                                  | 1       |
| 3 | cycle | 7 12 13 22 23                               | 1       |
| 4 | cycle | 21 29                                       | 3       |
| 5 | tree  | 26 25 19 17 27 20 31 34                     | 0, 2, 3 |
| 6 | tree  | 32 35 37 30 28 33                           | 3, 4    |
| 7 | cycle | 36 38 39                                    | 6       |
| 8 | tree  | 40                                          | 7       |

**Main cause: room 22 and its tree must sit inside a ring.** Rooms 12 and 23 are joined by three
disjoint paths: `22`, `7-13`, `21-29` (faces `7-12-22-23-13` and `22-12-21-29-23`). Without
corridors rooms touch, so by the Jordan curve theorem one path lies inside the ring the other two
form. Enclosing `7-13` would pull in everything behind hub 3, enclosing `21-29` the rooms of
chains 6–8; the cheapest is `22` with its pendant tree `31-34` (31 on 22, 34 on 31). Cycle chains 3
and 4 close the ring tight, so no cavity is left: over every state the planner reached chain 5
from (seeds 1 and 2, 20 states), room 31 has **0 free offsets** next to 22 (0/144 over all its
shapes and configuration space points), against 2–103 for the other tree rooms.

**Second cause: cycles before trees.** Chain 5 is a merged tree: leaves 26 and 25 hang on chain 0,
19/17/20 on chain 2. They wait for chains 1–4, which crowd chain 0 and 2 (some states leave 0/132
or 2/152 free offsets). Edgar does the same (`BreadthFirstChainDecomposition.cs`: any cycle
candidate beats any tree), so this is a faithful port, not a bug.

**Third: the ring-closing chain is short.** Chain 4 (`21,29`) gives a layout in ~5% of its tries
(105 nodes, 5 layouts on seed 1); the backtracking into chains 2–3 takes ~80% of the 500k
iterations.

Experiments on M-3:

| variant                                                         | M-3 | stuck on      |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------|-----|---------------|
| defaults                                                        | 0/5 | chain 5       |
| rooms 31, 34 removed                                            | 1/5 | trees on 0, 2 |
| tree parts placed as soon as they touch a placed room ("early") | 0/5 | `21,29`       |
| early, rooms 31, 34 removed                                     | 4/5 | `21,29`       |
| early, room 34 removed                                          | 0/5 | `21,29`       |
| chains 3 + 4 + rooms 31, 34 annealed as one chain               | 0/5 | that chain    |

"Early" over the whole bench: M-1 5/5 → 4/5, L-7 18 → 189 ms/seed, the rest pass. Not adopted.

So the step 8 note "M-3 is not short cycle chains" was half wrong: the blocker is the enclosed
pendant tree, made worse by the short ring-closing chain and by cycles-before-trees.

## Decision (2026-10-27)

M-3 needs a cavity in a ring, which a corridor-free layout of touching rooms cannot give by design.
Corridors (step 11) are Edgar's way to get slack: rooms of a ring sit apart and the gap holds what is
inside. M-3 waits for step 11 and is a known failure until then. No engine change in 8b.

## For step 11 (corridors)

- Acceptance: M-3 5/5 with corridors on, then remove it from `knownFailures`.
- Risk: corridors are a seeded per-door choice (`corridorRate`). The ring helps only if one of its
  doors (12-22, 22-23, 12-21, 21-29, 29-23, 12-7, 7-13, 13-23) or 22-31 gets a corridor. Decide at
  step 11 whether to measure M-3 over corridor seeds or to prefer corridors on face doors.
- If corridors are not enough: the "early trees" order (fixes the second cause, costs M-1 and L-7)
  and placing an enclosed pendant tree before its ring closes are the next candidates. Both are
  ours, not Edgar's.
